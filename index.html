Privacy Policy — Effective November 2026

Zero Telemetry. Zero Cloud. Privacy by Architecture.

MacBridge ("we", "our", or "the application") is a native, privacy-focused remote desktop application designed for macOS and iPadOS. We believe that your desktop, your keystrokes, your screen content, and your network topology belong exclusively to you.

1. Core Architectural Guarantees

  • No Data Collection: We do not collect, harvest, aggregate, sell, or rent any personal data, usage metrics, or diagnostic telemetry.
  • No Cloud Accounts: MacBridge operates without user accounts, cloud sign-ins, centralized databases, or relay servers operated by MacBridge.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: The application contains zero advertising SDKs, third-party analytics trackers, crash reporters, or behavioral monitors.
  • End-to-End Cryptography: All network communication—whether local over Bonjour or remote over peer-to-peer tunnels—is secured with Mutual TLS 1.3 (mTLS) with pinned cryptographic certificates and ephemeral session keys.

2. Information Handled by the Application

A. Screen Capture and Desktop Video

When an authenticated session is active, macOS ScreenCaptureKit captures display frames in memory. Frames are encoded in hardware using VideoToolbox (H.264) and sent directly over the authenticated mTLS socket to your iPad. Video packets are decoded and displayed in memory via AVSampleBufferDisplayLayer. Video frames are streamed transiently in real time and are never cached to disk, recorded, or transmitted to any third party.

B. Keyboard, Mouse, and Trackpad Input

Hardware keyboard, trackpad, mouse, and touchscreen inputs are captured via public iPadOS UIKit APIs, normalized into device-agnostic protocols, and transmitted over the encrypted connection. Keystrokes and mouse coordinates exist only in volatile memory for the duration of the stream. They are never logged or stored.

C. Device Cryptographic Identities

Each device generates a persistent 256-bit cryptographic identity (Ed25519 / P-256) locally upon initial launch. Private keys never leave your device and are stored securely in the hardware Secure Enclave or Keychain. Public keys are exchanged solely during explicit user-approved pairing.

D. Network Endpoints & Wake Credentials

Local IP addresses, MAC addresses, and user-configured remote DNS endpoints (e.g. Tailscale or WireGuard private hostnames) are stored strictly on your local device within the application sandbox.

3. Apple Privacy Manifest (NSPrivacy) Declarations

In compliance with Apple's privacy manifest requirements (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy):

  • NSPrivacyTracking: false (Zero tracking across apps and websites).
  • NSPrivacyTrackingDomains: Empty (No tracking domains contacted).
  • NSPrivacyAccessedAPITypeUserDefaults (Reason: CA92.1): Used exclusively for storing user preferences (paired device display names, wake timeout intervals, and pointer sensitivity) inside the local app sandbox.
  • NSPrivacyAccessedAPITypeSystemBootTime (Reason: 35F9.1): Used solely to measure local monotonic uptime and elapsed time across wake intervals and session heartbeats.

4. In-App Purchases & StoreKit 2

MacBridge offers an optional lifetime unlock for advanced remote access features. All transactions are handled exclusively through Apple's native StoreKit 2 framework. Apple processes your payment information and transaction receipts under the standard Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions. MacBridge does not collect payment details, credit card numbers, billing addresses, or user identifiers.

5. System Permissions

MacBridge requests only the minimum platform permissions required to operate:

  • macOS Screen Recording: Required by Apple to capture the desktop display via ScreenCaptureKit.
  • macOS Accessibility: Required to inject keyboard, mouse, and trackpad events into the desktop session.
  • iPadOS Local Network: Required by Apple to discover nearby Macs via Bonjour (_macbridge._tcp) and connect directly.

You may revoke any permission at any time through macOS System Settings or iPadOS Settings.
